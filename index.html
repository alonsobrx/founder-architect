About

I am Arnoldo Alonso, a U.S. Army OIF/OND combat veteran, former Staff Sergeant, founder, senior consultant, data scientist, and neuromorphic computing engineer.

I build systems where law, medicine, public policy, and technology meet. My work emphasizes explainable evidence, accountable human judgment, privacy, and access for blind users, disabled veterans, bilingual communities, and people working with limited connectivity.

Ayala is my neuromorphic and big-data architecture for legal-medical workflows. The veteran-claims work associated with Ayala has assisted more than 3,182 veterans since 2023.

Brasidas Strategies applies data science, behavioral analytics, economic research, and systems design to complex public-interest problems.

Operating Standards

  • Evidence before claims: sources, assumptions, calculations, and limitations should be inspectable.
  • Accessibility from the first commit: keyboard access, screen-reader support, plain language, and bilingual design are requirements.
  • Human accountability: automated systems should support decisions without hiding who is responsible.
  • Real-world resilience: tools should remain useful on older hardware, limited bandwidth, and interrupted connections.
  • Privacy by design: collect the minimum data necessary and keep sensitive work local whenever possible.
