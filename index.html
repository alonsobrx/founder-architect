Mission

I am Arnoldo Alonso, a U.S. Army OIF/OND combat veteran, former Staff Sergeant, co-founder and partner, data scientist, and neuromorphic computing engineer.

Military logistics taught me that a system is only real when it works under pressure. I apply that standard to legal-medical evidence, public policy, disability access, economic research, and technical infrastructure.

Ayala is my neuromorphic and big-data architecture for high-stakes evidence workflows. Brasidas Strategies applies data science, behavioral analytics, economic research, and systems design to public-interest problems.

The work is designed for blind users, disabled veterans, bilingual communities, older devices, limited bandwidth, interrupted connectivity, and institutions where human accountability cannot be automated away.

Operating Standards

  • Evidence before claims: identify sources, assumptions, calculations, dates, and limitations.
  • Accessibility from the first commit: keyboard access, screen-reader structure, plain language, and bilingual routes are requirements.
  • Human accountability: automated systems may support judgment without hiding who remains responsible.
  • Real-world resilience: tools should remain useful on older hardware, limited bandwidth, and interrupted connections.
  • Privacy by architecture: collect the minimum data necessary and keep sensitive work local whenever possible.
