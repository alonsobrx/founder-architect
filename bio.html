Professional Identity

Arnoldo Alonso is a U.S. Army OIF/OND combat veteran and former Staff Sergeant (E-6) who served for thirteen years. As a 92A Automated Logistical Specialist, he worked in sustainment, accountability, recovery, customs, and redeployment operations during the drawdown from Iraq.

His military experience established the operational standard that guides his engineering: systems must remain understandable, accountable, and useful under pressure.

He is the founder of Ayala and co-founder and partner at Brasidas Strategies. His work combines data science, neuromorphic computing, behavioral analytics, economic research, and legal-medical systems strategy.

The veteran-claims work associated with Ayala has assisted more than 3,182 veterans since 2023. The system is designed to support evidence organization and human review; it does not replace an accredited representative, attorney, clinician, or authorized decision-maker.

Engineering Method

His engineering priorities are accessibility, privacy, low-bandwidth operation, and accountable human review. He designs for blind users, disabled veterans, bilingual communities, and people navigating institutions without reliable technical or legal support.

His public-interest research focuses on disability adjudication, veteran access, economic policy, institutional accountability, and technology that can be audited rather than merely trusted.
