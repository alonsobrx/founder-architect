Biography

Arnoldo Alonso is a U.S. Army OIF/OND combat veteran and former Staff Sergeant (E-6) who served for thirteen years. As a 92A Automated Logistical Specialist, he worked in sustainment, accountability, recovery, and redeployment operations during the drawdown from Iraq.

He is the founder of Ayala and Brasidas Strategies. His work combines data science, neuromorphic computing, behavioral analytics, and legal-medical systems strategy. The veteran-claims work associated with Ayala has assisted more than 3,182 veterans since 2023.

His engineering priorities are accessibility, privacy, low-bandwidth operation, and accountable human review. He designs for blind users, disabled veterans, bilingual communities, and people navigating institutions without reliable technical or legal support.

His public-interest research focuses on disability adjudication, veteran access, economic policy, institutional accountability, and technology that can be audited rather than merely trusted.
