Publications

Research should identify its sources, assumptions, methods, limitations, and revision status.

Publication Standard

  • Separate verified facts from forecasts, estimates, commentary, and recommendations.
  • Link to primary documents whenever they are available.
  • Date every analysis and publish corrections when later events change the record.
  • State when a document is a draft, archive, or work undergoing citation review.
