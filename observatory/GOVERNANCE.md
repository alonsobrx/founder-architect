# Governance

## Status

The observatory is an independent public-interest research project in preview. Arnoldo A. Alonso is the initial maintainer and publication authority. Veterans Defense Fund is an initiative in development and is not represented here as an incorporated nonprofit.

## Decision standard

Material changes must preserve four distinctions: Official, Calculated, Scenario, and Unknown. A conclusion may change when better evidence appears; the source record and change history must show why.

## Review gates

A public release should pass four separate reviews:

1. Data and economic-method review.
2. Legal-language and privacy review.
3. Accessibility review, including manual disabled-user testing.
4. Spanish-language review by a qualified human reviewer.

One person may initially perform more than one role, but the record must identify which reviews were completed and which remain pending.

## Corrections

Corrections are evidence-driven, not viewpoint-driven. A correction request must identify the disputed statement, authoritative source, exact location, and downstream calculations affected. Material corrections require a changelog entry and version increment.

## Independence and conflicts

Contributors must disclose financial, employment, client, or litigation interests that could reasonably affect the work. No donor, vendor, political organization, government agency, or contractor receives editorial control in exchange for support.

## Publication states

- Research preview: direct-link publication with `noindex` and unresolved review gates disclosed.
- Public release: indexing may be enabled after the four review gates are documented.
- Archived release: retained for traceability and never silently replaced.
