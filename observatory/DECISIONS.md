# Observatory Decision Record

## Decision 2026-08-10-01: Preview ownership and approved scenario defaults

Decision date: August 10, 2026

Responsible decision-maker: Arnoldo A. Alonso

Status: approved for the direct-link research preview; independent review remains required before an indexed public release.

## Approved public identity

The publication will use:

> Veterans Benefits Capacity and Due Process Observatory  
> An independent public-interest research initiative directed by Arnoldo A. Alonso.

Arnoldo A. Alonso is the public research director and responsible preview publisher.

Veterans Defense Fund may be described only as a public-interest initiative in development unless incorporation, nonprofit, trademark, fundraising, or other legal status is documented and separately approved for publication.

## Approved public contact

The preview will use Arnoldo A. Alonso’s public LinkedIn profile as the ordinary contact route. It will not publish a private telephone number, private email address, veteran-assistance intake channel, or confidential reporting details.

Sensitive vulnerability reports must follow the repository security policy and must not include exploit details or protected information in a public issue or LinkedIn message.

## Approved economic scenario defaults

The following values are approved as editable planning scenarios, not as VA-published facts:

1. Target operating utilization: 85 percent.
2. Dedicated annual inventory reduction: 100,000 matters.
3. Marginal annual production per added VBA full-time-equivalent employee: 225 matters.
4. Fully loaded annual cost per added VBA employee: $180,000.
5. Share of annual rating matters receiving intensive claimant-side assistance: 25 percent.
6. Intensive matters per claimant-side professional per year: 85.
7. Fully loaded annual cost per claimant-side professional: $160,000.

The public interface and data files must continue to label these values as scenarios. Approval authorizes their use in the preview; it does not substitute for independent economics, public-administration, or congressional scoring review.

## Approved publication posture

The Observatory will remain:

- publicly available by direct link;
- labeled as an independent research preview;
- excluded from search-engine indexing through `noindex, nofollow`;
- hosted in `alonsobrx/founder-architect/observatory` during the stabilization period; and
- separate from confidential veteran systems and proprietary Ayala technology.

A dedicated GitHub organization, permanent repository, custom domain, donation capability, or formal organizational claim requires a later documented decision.

## Approved security posture

Security is treated as a release gate rather than a marketing claim. The repository must maintain:

- least-privilege and immutable GitHub Actions;
- automated secret, privacy, workflow, and static-site checks;
- CodeQL analysis;
- CODEOWNERS and pull-request review controls;
- a documented threat model and incident-response process;
- separation of public research from confidential veteran data and proprietary systems; and
- explicit disclosure of controls that still require manual GitHub account or repository settings.

No statement may promise that the project is invulnerable, certified, or free of all defects.

## Decisions still open

The following are not approved by this record:

- the exact legal status of Veterans Defense Fund;
- any representation that it is incorporated, tax-exempt, VA-accredited, or authorized to solicit charitable donations;
- a custom domain or DNS owner;
- publication of a final petition or exhibits;
- removal of `noindex, nofollow`;
- an indexed v0.2 release;
- named independent reviewers; or
- publication of confidential, personally identifying, medical, claims, financial, attorney-client, proprietary, or patent-sensitive information.

## Change control

A material change to ownership, legal status, contact methods, model defaults, data classification, privacy boundary, security architecture, indexing status, or release authority requires a new dated decision record and review through a pull request.
