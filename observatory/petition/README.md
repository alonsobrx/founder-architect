# Living Petition Publication Control

## Current status

A final legal petition is not published in version 0.1.0. The live site publishes a source-backed policy framework and capacity models, not a signed pleading, legal conclusion, or personal claim file.

## Release requirements

Before a petition version becomes public, it must have:

1. A fixed version number and publication date.
2. Legal review of jurisdiction, requested relief, factual allegations, and protected speech.
3. A source citation for every material factual assertion.
4. Clear labels for calculations, scenarios, and unknowns.
5. Removal of personal medical, claim, contact, credential, and identifying information.
6. Authoritative accessible HTML, with PDF only as a secondary export.
7. A human-reviewed Spanish version when represented as bilingual.
8. A changelog and permanent archived copy.

## Prohibited submissions

GitHub Issues and pull requests are not claim-intake channels. Do not submit medical evidence, VA records, Social Security numbers, signatures, addresses, or confidential attorney-client material.
